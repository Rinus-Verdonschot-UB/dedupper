Transparency

How Dedupper decides.

Every pair of records Dedupper compares gets one of the rules below. The rule ID is shown next to each duplicate group, review item, related publication and near miss, and it is written into the decision log and audit JSON.

Decision outcomes

  • duplicate: proposed for a duplicate group. You choose what to keep; automatic mode keeps the recommended copy.
  • related: different publication versions of the same work (preprint, conference abstract, protocol, notice…). Both are kept unless you choose otherwise.
  • review: an ambiguous match that is never removed automatically.
  • separate: compared and kept apart; listed as a near miss.

Publication versions

Each record is labelled from, in order of strength: correction/retraction/comment markers in the title; the publication or document type (RIS PT/DT/M3, PubMed publication types, mapped CSV/Excel column); preprint-server DOI prefixes (bioRxiv/medRxiv, arXiv, SSRN, Research Square, OSF, ChemRxiv and others); preprint-server or trial-registry source names and links; protocol and conference-abstract title markers; and RIS types CONF, CPAPER and THES. The evidence is shown next to every label.
